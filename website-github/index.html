Joe Holschuh

Joe Holschuh (also known as Joseph Holschuh, or Mr. Holschuh to a couple decades of students) is an English teacher, podcaster, crossword constructor, and commencement speaker based in Kimberly, Wisconsin. He co-hosts the literary podcast You Don't Know Lit, directs the Kimberly High School Writing Center, builds educational web tools under the RoboHolschuh banner, and delivers the occasional commencement address.
